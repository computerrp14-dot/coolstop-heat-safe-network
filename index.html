            Heat safety

            Practical steps for outdoor workers during extreme heat.

            Heat risk depends on local temperature, humidity, wind, direct sun, workload and personal factors. Always follow your workplace heat-safety plan and official local guidance.

            Everyday habits

            • 🟠 Take short, frequent rest breaks in shade — better than one long break.
            • 🟠 Drink water regularly, before you feel thirsty.
            • 🟠 Wear light, loose clothing and a hat.
            • 🟠 Schedule the heaviest tasks earlier or later when possible.
            • 🟠 Watch out for colleagues.

            Recognise heat illness

            • 🔴 Heat exhaustion: heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, nausea, headache, muscle cramps.
            • 🔴 Heat stroke (emergency): hot dry skin OR heavy sweating, confusion, slurred speech, fainting, seizures.
            • 🔴 Move to shade, cool the person, give water if conscious.
            ⚠ If you suspect heat stroke, call emergency services immediately.

            About CoolStop & impact

            Why we built this, who it helps, and how it relates to the competition theme.

            The problem

            Outdoor workers spend long hours in extreme heat with few places to rest. Shade, drinking water and cooling rooms are often missing, hard to find, or unmarked.

            Our solution

            CoolStop maps nearby relief facilities and lets workers report where shade, water or cooling is missing, so employers can prioritise action.

            Intended users

            • • Outdoor workers looking for shade, water or a rest point.
            • • Site supervisors planning rest schedules.
            • • Municipal teams and NGOs mapping heat-relief gaps.

            Impact

            • • Fewer heat-related illnesses.
            • • Better use of existing relief facilities.
            • • Data for tree planting and water-point planning.

            Theme link

            “Super El Niño: Protect Nature, Protect Life” highlights how extreme climate patterns threaten both ecosystems and the people who depend on them. CoolStop focuses on the human side — outdoor workers are among the first to feel extreme heat.

            Limitations

            • • All facilities, heat-risk values and starting reports are invented demonstration data.
            • • Reports are stored only in your browser and are not sent anywhere.
            • • Heat-risk examples are not official alerts.
            • • Priority engine is a rule-based prototype, not ML.
